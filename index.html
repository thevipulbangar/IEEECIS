SEC/02 · ABOUT
SYS/02 CONFERENCE
INTRODUCTION
NOV 2026

THE NEXT ERA OF INTELLIGENCE

FUTURETECH 2026 is the flagship gathering of the IEEE Computational Intelligence Society for the region. Three days of peer-reviewed research, industry practice and open argument about where machine intelligence is actually going — not where the press releases say it is.

The programme is built for people who write the papers and ship the systems. Every main track pairs a research session with a practitioner response, so findings are pressure-tested in the room rather than six months later.

01 / RESEARCH

Peer-reviewed depth

Double-blind review across six tracks, with proceedings submitted for IEEE Xplore indexing.

02 / INDUSTRY

Practice in the room

Roundtables with engineering teams deploying these methods under real latency, cost and safety limits.

03 / COMMUNITY

Careers, not just talks

Doctoral consortium, mentoring tables and chapter meets that continue after the conference closes.

WHY ATTEND
  • Nine research domains under one roof, from foundation models to neuromorphic silicon.
  • Hybrid by design — every main-track session streams with live moderated Q&A.
  • Student rates from ₹999, with travel grants for presenting authors.
  • Proceedings, recordings and datasets released to delegates within 72 hours.

SYS/09 · IEEE CIS

BUILT ON A GLOBAL RESEARCH COMMUNITY

THE SOCIETY

The IEEE Computational Intelligence Society advances the theory, design and application of biologically and linguistically motivated computation — neural networks, fuzzy systems and evolutionary computation among them. Its members work across universities, national labs and industry in more than a hundred countries.

COLLABORATION

Technical committees, task forces and local chapters connect researchers who would otherwise never share a room. FUTURETECH 2026 is organised by the regional chapter network, with the programme committee drawn from eleven countries and reviewed double-blind.

WHY IT MATTERS

Computational intelligence is where methods get tested against problems that resist clean formulation. A conference is the mechanism that keeps that testing honest: work is presented, questioned and revised in public, then published for anyone to check.
